WELCOME TO THE TABLE

How to play Suipi

Each player gets eight cards. Four cards start on the table. Play one hand card per turn.

01 · Capture

Match a table card, or take cards whose values add up to your hand card. A 9 can take a 5 and a 4. Select all cards you want to take.

02 · Build & group

Add your hand card to table cards, keeping a card that can capture the result. Combine equal-value builds into a locked group. The table shows each pile’s value and owner. Your opponent can capture your pile.

03 · Drop

Place a card on the table when legal. You cannot drop a matching table value or drop while you own a build.

04 · Suipi!

Capture every card on the table to earn a sweep. After three deals, remaining table cards go to the last player to capture.

Points

Most cards: 3 · 10♦: 2 · 2♠: 1 · Most spades: 1 · Each ace: 1. The engine awards the difference in sweep counts as a bonus to the player with more sweeps. Tied card counts earn no majority points.

Play two games, taking turns as dealer. Highest combined score wins. Scores during a game are provisional.
