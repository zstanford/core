FIELD GUIDE

Fuse energy. Capture points.

52 shapes: energies 1–13, four color families. Four shared starters are dealt once. Each player gets eight shapes per round, over three rounds. No replacement draws during a round.

Capture

Match the same energy, or capture sums. A 7 can capture 3 + 4. Energies 11, 12, and 13 add normally. Original captured shapes—not energy—determine scoring.

Forge and lock

Add shapes and morph them into the resulting solid, up to 13. Keep a matching finisher in your arsenal. Rivals may raise unlocked builds or capture any build. Equal builds group into a locked value: two 7-builds remain a locked 7. Ownership rings are separate from shape color.

Original identities stay inside

A gold 2 plus crystal 10 forms a 12 with three special points. It does not become a new crystal 12 worth four. Select formations to inspect their source shapes, colors, and stored points.

Deploy

Leave a shape in shared space. You cannot deploy a matching field energy or deploy while owning a build. Each move uses one shape from your hand.

23 points, plus sweeps

Most captured shapes: 3. Most gold shapes: 1. Gold 2: 1. Every energy-1 shape: 1. Crystal 10 / 11 / 12 / 13: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5. A full battlefield sweep adds 1. Central diamonds show crystal point values; small digits show energy.

The battle line carries forward

The line follows the current point lead, including contested capture and gold majorities. A round ends after both hands empty. Its winner is whoever leads then. Points and battlefield formations carry forward. Play all three rounds; most round wins takes the match. Drawn rounds stay draws; tied round wins use final point lead. Equal final points draw the match.

Final pickup

Only after round three, leftover field shapes go to the last capturer before final scoring. Pickup alone earns no sweep. Initial starters exclude point shapes and duplicate energies. Tied capture or gold counts award no majority points.

Drag a shape from your 3D hand onto a field formation to capture or forge. The active action takes priority when both are legal. Tap several field formations first to include them in a drop. Invalid drops return to your hand without using a turn. Drag empty space to orbit. You can also select shapes with the labeled controls. Small numbers brighten on selection.
