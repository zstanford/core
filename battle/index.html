FIELD GUIDE

Fuse shapes. Capture points.

52 shapes: values 1–13, four color families. Four shared starters are dealt once. Each player gets eight shapes per round, over three rounds. No replacement draws during a round.

Capture

Match the same value, or capture sums. A 7 can capture 3 + 4. Values 11, 12, and 13 add normally. Original captured shapes determine scoring; higher values do not automatically earn more points.

Fuse and stack

Add shapes and morph them into the resulting solid, up to 13. Keep a matching finisher in your hand. Rivals may raise unlocked formations or capture any formation. Stack equal-value combinations into a locked formation: two 7-formations remain a locked 7. Ownership rings are separate from shape color.

Original identities stay inside

A gold 2 plus crystal 10 forms a 12 with three special points. It does not become a new crystal 12 worth four. Select formations to inspect their source shapes, colors, and stored points.

Place

Leave a shape on the field. A drop automatically previews a capture when field values match or add up to your shape’s value. Otherwise, it previews placing the shape. You cannot place a matching field value or place while owning a formation. Each move uses one shape from your hand.

23 points, plus sweeps

Most captured shapes: 3. Most gold shapes: 1. Gold 2: 1. Every value-1 shape: 1. Crystal 10 / 11 / 12 / 13: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5. A Sweep clears the entire field and adds 1 point. Central diamonds show crystal point values; small digits show value.

The battle line carries forward

The line follows the current point lead, including contested capture and gold majorities. A round ends after both hands empty. Its winner is whoever leads then. Points and field formations carry forward. Play all three rounds; most round wins takes the match. Drawn rounds stay draws; tied round wins use final point lead. Equal final points draw the match.

Final pickup

Only after round three, leftover field shapes go to the last capturer before final scoring. Pickup alone earns no sweep. Initial starters exclude point shapes and duplicate values. Tied capture or gold counts award no majority points.

Drag a shape from your 3D hand onto a field formation to capture or fuse. When you can either capture or fuse, choose the result in the confirmation bar. Drop onto empty field space to preview a capture automatically, or place the shape if no capture is available. Confirm commits your move; Undo returns the staged shape without using a turn. Tap several field formations first to include them in a drop. Invalid drops return to your hand without using a turn. Drag empty space to rotate the view. You can also select shapes with the labeled controls. Small numbers brighten on selection. Sentinel chooses using its own hand and the public field.
