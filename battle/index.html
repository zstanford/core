FIELD GUIDE

The table is now a battlefield.

Every solid has fixed energy. Geometry identifies the tier; larger solids hit harder. Face count does not define energy.

Strike · capture

Match energy, or combine smaller formations. Tetra (3) + Cube (4) can be captured by Crown (7). A strike deals twice your shape’s energy.

Forge · build and group

Add your shape to the field, keeping a matching finisher in your arsenal. Lock equal-value builds together with the same finisher. Opponents can hijack formations, including locked ones; locked values cannot change.

Deploy · discard

Leave a shape in shared space. You cannot deploy a matching field energy or deploy while owning a formation.

Suipi · sweep

Clear every field formation during a strike for 8 extra damage. Each turn draws a replacement while supplies last. Destroy the rival core, or finish with more hull when both arsenals run out.

Sentinel uses its own arsenal and the public field to choose legal moves. Drag the arena to orbit, scroll to zoom, or use the labeled controls. Sound starts off. Motion follows your device preference.
